The KPBR Heritage Story

A Secunderabad Landmark Woven Into Family Memories

For over five decades, Kotha Pentaiah & Bodla Ramachandraiah Banaras Silk Palace has stood as more than a textile showroom—it is a trusted family institution in the bustling heart of General Bazaar, Secunderabad.

We believe choosing a wedding saree or festive ensemble is an emotional milestone that deserves unhurried attention. Our seasoned staff and management take pride in patiently unfolding weave after weave—explaining zari purity, loom origin, and drape weight—so every family leaves with confidence and joy.
